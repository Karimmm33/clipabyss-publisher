Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who this covers

ClipAbyss Publisher is a private tool used by the owner of the ClipAbyss TikTok account (@clipzabyss) to upload videos to that account. It has no public users.

2. What data the tool handles

  • Basic profile information of the authorised TikTok account (such as its display name and identifier), received from TikTok after the owner grants permission.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, which let the tool publish to that account.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

The tool does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

3. How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account and to check that a video was published.

4. Where it is kept

Tokens and settings are stored on a private server controlled by the account owner. They are not sold, shared or sent to anyone other than TikTok.

5. How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until the owner withdraws permission or removes them. Videos that have been published stay on TikTok under TikTok's own policies.

6. Withdrawing permission and deleting data

Permission can be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. After that the stored tokens no longer work, and the owner deletes them from the server.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

8. Contact

Message the ClipAbyss account on TikTok: @clipzabyss.
